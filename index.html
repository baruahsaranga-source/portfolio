Who I Am

About me

I'm Saranga Baruah, a technology and business leader based in Noida, India. For over twenty years I've worked at the point where marketing operations meet engineering, building the platforms that let enterprise brands see, manage and improve what happens in the field.

At Multiplier Brand Solutions I founded the Tech & Data Solutions division and grew it into a profitable P&L centre with a 20-person team. Before that, a decade at DDB Mudra Group (Omnicom) taught me how to translate messy, large-scale trade marketing problems into software people actually use: HRMS platforms, CRM systems, BI dashboards and the SFA tool that went on to win global recognition.

What I enjoy most is the translation work: sitting with a C-suite problem, mapping the process underneath it, and shipping an automated, data-driven answer. Outside work you'll find me quizzing, following cricket, or out on a run or a ride.
